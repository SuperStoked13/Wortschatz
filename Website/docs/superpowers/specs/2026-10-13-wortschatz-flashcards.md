# Wortschatz (Mac): Karteikarten (flashcards)

Date: 2026-10-13. Replaces the set page's "Nur offene" shortcut (and "Nur offene schreiben" in the "…" menu).

## Decisions (user)

- Quizlet-style flashcards with "Fortschritt verfolgen": the learner turns a card over and sorts it into **Weiss ich** (→) or **Lerne ich noch** (←). At the end of a round the "Lerne ich noch" cards come again, until every card is known.
- Sorting **does not count** towards *Gemeistert*. Mastery stays with Lernen and Schreiben, which actually test the learner.
- The card turns over with a **3D flip** around the horizontal axis, with a light sweeping across the glass. Revised the same day for more depth: the flip lifts the card and turns it once, stopping flat (an overshoot was tried and removed at the user's request), and sorting seals the card and glides it off to its side (see Design › Motion). A pointer tilt with a glass highlight was tried and removed at the user's request; the first glide (wind-up, turn in depth) was replaced by a calmer one because it looked jittery.
- "Nur offene" disappears everywhere (button and menu item).
- Added later the same day: the card can be **swiped** with two fingers on the trackpad (or dragged with the mouse) to "Lerne ich noch" (left) or "Weiss ich" (right); it springs back when let go too early.

## Behaviour

- Entry points: the "Karteikarten" button after "Schreiben" on the set page, Lernset › Karteikarten (⌥⌘L), and the set context menu (sidebar, overview). "…" › "Karteikarten neu starten" appears while a deck is under way. A running deck shows a line under the buttons: "Karteikarten: Runde 2 · 8 von 12 gewusst".
- Screen: the same study layout as Lernen/Schreiben (the sidebar slides away, the screen fades in, "‹ Lernsets" and esc lead back).
- Keys: space / Return / ↑↓ turn the card; ← Lerne ich noch; → Weiss ich; ⌫ brings the last card back (also from the round summary); after a round Return = "Weiter mit N Karten"; at the end Return = "Nochmal von vorne". Lernen menu: ⌘K Optionen, ⌘R Vorlesen, ⌘D Markieren, ⇧⌘R Neu starten, esc Beenden. ⌘E edits the card.
- Options (⌘K): Vorderseite (term or definition language) and Mischen. Both apply to every set (UserDefaults `flashcards.front`, `flashcards.shuffle`), also to a deck started before the change; with Mischen off the next round comes in the set's order. "Nur markierte" restarts the deck with the starred cards.
- Summary after a round ("Du machst Fortschritte!") and at the end ("Super, du kennst alle N Karten!", with confetti, plus a button "Lernen" to test the cards for real).
- Sounds (subtle, like Lernen; Settings › Allgemein › Soundeffekte, `SoundEffects`): turning a card over is silent (a swish was tried and removed at the user's request), "Weiss ich" the correct chime a little softer with a swish up (`know`), "Lerne ich noch" a warm E5 → D5 with a swish down (`stillLearning`, deliberately not the low tone of a miss), "Zurück" falling air (`undo`). The last card of a round plays the round's fanfare instead. The swishes are filtered noise synthesized like the chimes (`Synth.Swish`), well below them in level.
- The deck follows set changes: deleted cards leave it, new cards join at the end of the round, and a starred-only deck keeps cards that lose their star.

## Storage

The decks are not progress, so they are not in the library. They live in `library.flashcards.json` beside the library file (a UI-test library `<name>.json` gets `<name>.flashcards.json`). In-memory libraries keep them in memory. Writes are coalesced (400 ms) and flushed by `AppModel.save()`. Inconsistent decks, and decks of deleted sets, are dropped when the file is read.

## Design

- One large Liquid Glass card in the study column, sized like an index card (240–440 pt high). The language name sits at the top left, 🔊 ★ ✎ at the top right, the text is centred (`Typography.flashcard`), and a quiet "␣ Umdrehen" hint is at the bottom. The back has a faint accent tint.
- Stack: at rest, two card edges peek out below the card (only the part below the card shows, so the glass is not clouded). While the card turns, the stack's cards show at their full size behind it (fully from about 40° of the turn on), and are back under it by the time the glass returns. A stack card hides the one behind it, so their tints never add up.
- The action bar (glass, one container) shows ↶ Zurück, then "← ↺ Lerne ich noch [n]", "✓ Weiss ich [n] →", and "Umdrehen ↩". In narrow windows the labels give way to the symbols.

### Motion (`FlashcardCard.swift`, `FlashcardMotion`)

- **Glass and crystal.** Liquid Glass cannot be turned: under a 2D rotation or any perspective, macOS draws the glass as a larger, misplaced slab beside the content (only moving and scaling keep it in place; checked with snapshots, and the first flip had the same fault). So the resting card is real Liquid Glass, flat and empty, with the content drawn over it. While the card turns (more than a few tenths of a degree) or leaves, a crystal stand-in drawn by SwiftUI takes over: a translucent pane matched to the resting glass on the study backdrop (brighter middle, light rim strongest at the top, a soft shadow outside the pane only), so the switch does not show.
- **Smoothness.** The card's two sides are built once; every frame of a turn or a glide only recomputes small animatable modifiers (`FaceTurn`, `GlassFade`, `GlideEffect`), never the content.
- Flip: the card turns around a nearly horizontal axis (leaning 0.12, as if turned by hand), always the same way round. Halfway, edge-on, it is lifted toward the viewer (+4.5 %) and 10 pt up. It turns once and stops exactly flat (0.62 s, a bezier without overshoot: no swinging past flat and back, at the user's request). A streak of light with a bright core rises across the card while it turns (gentler in light mode); it stays on the card (from its lower part to its upper third) and has faded out exactly when the card lies flat, so it is never cut off by an edge while still bright. The crystal stays on until the card lies flat and then dissolves into the real glass (0.32 s): before, the glass came back in the last ~80 ms of the turn and made the end feel abrupt. The stack behind it comes into view at full size while it turns (driven by the same animated angle as the card, `FlashcardMotion.stackReveal`); only strips under a card standing on its edge looked odd.
- Sorting: the card itself (the same view, because glass that appears anew flashes) becomes crystal at once while it still stands, floods green or amber from the side it is going to (with a coloured rim), and the verdict settles behind the text as a translucent seal (symbol in a ring, from slightly larger, no bounce) that sends out a ring. After a beat (60 ms) it glides off in one calm movement (0.5 s, gathering pace evenly, no wind-up and no turn in depth): 280 pt to its side, 24 pt down, leaning 6°, 6 % smaller, fading from about a third of the way on. A card swiped a little to one side and then sorted to the other by key glides across the middle (the glide is one signed value). The next card rises 16 pt from the stack (from 96.5 %); sorted mid-turn, the stack the turn uncovered goes back under it. "Zurück" sends a card in flight back along its own path; a card already gone slides back in from its side (moving and scaling only; from the round summary, once the last card's exit is over, it rises with the deck instead), and the current card sinks back under the stack. A card called back keeps its crystal until it is in place, then the crystal dissolves into the glass (0.32 s), as after a turn. A new round or a restart makes new views for its cards (keyed by the round), so a card still leaving finishes its exit.
- No pointer effects (a hover tilt and light were tried and removed).
- **Swiping** (`FlashcardSwipe.swift`): two-finger horizontal scroll events (a local event monitor of the window; a gesture that starts mostly sideways belongs to the card until the fingers lift, its momentum included, any other one scrolls the page) or a mouse drag (`highPriorityGesture`, so a click still turns the card) move the current card sideways. It moves on the sorted card's glide (leaning, a little smaller, dropping) but does not fade under the fingers, is crystal while it moves, and takes on the colour of the side it is heading for (a wash growing with the swipe). The card follows freely up to 170 pt and then reluctantly (rubber band). Past 110 pt the verdict's seal settles on it with a haptic tick (Force Touch trackpad); let go there, or flung (where it would be 0.14 s later decides), it flies off from where it is (`fling`, 0.42 s, fast start, slows down as it fades) and is sorted like with the arrow keys; else it springs back (`snapBack`, 0.36 s, no overshoot) and its crystal then dissolves into the glass. The stack behind is uncovered as the card moves aside and goes back under the next card as it rises. Reduce Motion: the card only moves sideways and fades out where it was let go. Snapshot scenes `flashcard-swipe-know` (armed) and `flashcard-swipe-learning`.
- Reduce Motion: the sides cross-fade on the glass, the cards only fade, nothing turns or glides, the seal only fades. Reduce Transparency: the crystal is opaque like the glass.
- Snapshot scenes `flashcard-*` (DEBUG, `--render-snapshots <dir> --snapshot-filter flashcard-`) freeze the motion at rest (glass), as crystal at rest (to hold against the glass), mid-flip (40°, 75°, 130°, 160°, with the stack showing), sealed and mid-glide.
